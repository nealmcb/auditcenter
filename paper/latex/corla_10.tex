\subsection*{Abstract}
{\it Placeholder abstract for this page-count mockup -- not yet drafted
for real submission.} Colorado has run a mandatory, statewide
risk-limiting audit after every election since 2017. We reconstruct,
clean, and normalize ten years of the state's public audit exports into a
single dataset, verify it against an independent ground truth, and report
the resulting structural coverage and discrepancy findings.
